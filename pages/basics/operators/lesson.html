Introduction to Operators

Operators let you compare values, combine conditions, and perform calculations. In this category, you will build expressions and inspect their results.

Compare and combine

const isOlder = 18 >= 18;
const canEnter = isOlder && true;
console.log(canEnter);

Comparisons produce Boolean values. Logical expressions combine those values to make a larger condition.

Calculate values

const total = 5 + 3;
const remainder = 10 % 3;
console.log(total);
console.log(remainder);
